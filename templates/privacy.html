Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

Spendly ("the Service") is operated by Maxmillian. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our personal expense tracking service. By using Spendly, you consent to the practices described in this policy. We may update this policy from time to posted here with a revised "last updated" date.

Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve the Service. This includes the account details you provide (name and email address) and the expense data you enter — such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect your location, contacts, or any data from third-party apps unless you explicitly choose to connect them.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to deliver the Service: storing your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and keeping your account secure. We use the email address you register with to authenticate you and communicate account-related messages. We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Data Storage

Your expense data and account information are stored on servers controlled by us or our trusted hosting providers. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We retain your data for as long as your account is active, and we delete it upon request or when your account is closed.

Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party service providers to help operate the Service — for example, hosting infrastructure or analytics tools. These providers are contractually bound to handle your data only as necessary to perform their services and in accordance with this policy. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or partners.

Your Choices

You can access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. If you request account deletion, your data will be removed from the Service, except where we are required by law to retain it for a limited period.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at the contact information provided on the Spendly website. We will respond to your inquiry as promptly as we can.
